The Art of Francella · redesign concept

The website
becomes a place.

The creative objective is not to make the current store prettier. It is to reproduce the emotional experience of entering Francella's gallery, private tattoo studio and Dreadful Things—then deliberately guide that wonder toward collecting, booking, shopping and visiting.

01 · The idea

Digital gallery.
Real business.

The physical space is the reference model. Darkness creates depth. Intentional pools of light create focus. Artwork and objects emerge rather than compete. Motion is slow enough to feel atmospheric, never gimmicky.

Atmosphere

Wonder

Create curiosity before asking for action. The site unfolds like moving deeper into the shop/gallery.

Authenticity

Only Francella

Francella, her artwork, her tattoos, her exhibition, her shop and her objects. No generic tattoo artist. No fake mystical imagery.

Conversion

Every room works

Each experience has a clear destination: collect art, submit a tattoo idea, buy an object, or walk through the physical door.

Wonder→Attention→Trust→Desire→Action

03 · Commerce architecture

Keep Shopify.
Change discovery.

Nothing in the concept requires throwing away her existing store engine. Shopify can retain products, variants, cart, checkout and fulfillment integrations. The customer simply reaches those functions through a more intentional front-end hierarchy.

Artwork / collection→Story→Related objects→Shopify product→Cart / checkout→Existing fulfillment
Preserve

Catalog

Products, variants, pricing, inventory and current ecommerce data.

Preserve

Fulfillment

Print-on-demand / supplier integrations remain operational underneath.

Replace

Showroom

Navigation, product discovery, merchandising, storytelling, atmosphere and hierarchy.

04 · Creative rules

She will notice.
So we must mean it.

Francella is both an artist and a business owner. Anything visually dishonest weakens the pitch. These rules are part of the design system, not suggestions.

1
Portraits are Francella.

No invented artist stand-ins, no generic “tattoo woman,” no AI likeness.

2
Artwork is Francella's work.

No AI approximations of her paintings and no decorative stock art competing with them.

3
Tattoos are Francella's tattoos.

The portfolio is evidence of caliber. Generic tattoo imagery destroys that evidence.

4
The environment is the real environment.

Dreadful Things, the studio, exhibition and objects become our lighting and spatial reference once original photos are supplied.

5
Every visual does business work.

Establish identity, prove caliber, build desire, explain the world, or move someone toward collect / book / shop / visit.

Working proof

Walk through it.

The homepage and internal rooms are clickable. Prototype-only actions show deliberate demo messages instead of errors.
